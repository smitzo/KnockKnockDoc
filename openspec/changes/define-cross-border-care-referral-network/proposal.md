## Why

Cross-border patients and their referring clinicians currently coordinate high-stakes Indian treatment through fragmented email, messaging, hospital forms, agents, and spreadsheets. The opportunity is not another directory: it is a consent-led referral and case-coordination network that gives patients, referring doctors, Indian clinicians, and hospital international-patient teams one accountable workflow while leaving diagnosis and treatment decisions with licensed clinicians.

The premise remains unproven. Major hospitals already provide opinions, visa letters, and concierge services, and existing facilitators already compare providers. Before software development, the founders must validate a narrow source-country and specialty corridor, hospitals' willingness to pay for workflow and qualified demand, foreign doctors' willingness and legal ability to participate, and a monetization model that does not distort referrals.

## What Changes

- Position the product as a verified professional referral network, not a medical-tourism listing, generic CRM, or doctor social network.
- Define one closed-loop transaction: specialist discovery, consented referral, structured records, hospital review, attributable opinion/proposal, patient decision, and follow-up handback.
- Define four minimum surfaces: professional doctor network, patient case workspace, hospital international-patient CRM, and human platform operations.
- Make doctors free participants and Indian hospitals the initial paying customer through a bounded corridor operating pilot.
- Select one source-country and specialty corridor using evidence rather than launching globally.
- Define a first-10-case V0, manual-versus-software boundary, screens, acceptance scenario, network loop, commercial proof, bottom-up economics, and GTM implications.
- Preserve the earlier consent, permission, verification, and coordination constraints without allowing generic compliance work to dominate V0.
- Exclude diagnosis, prescribing, public doctor ranking, social feeds, broad travel commerce, payments custody, referral kickbacks, global/all-specialty scope, and premature automation.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `verified-professional-network`: Evidence-backed doctor/hospital profiles, focused discovery, lightweight professional relationships, and spam-resistant communication boundaries.
- `referral-case-workspace`: Doctor- or patient-originated referral, patient consent, records, isolated provider requests, case collaboration, attributable responses, decision, and handback.
- `hospital-international-crm`: Qualified-case inbox, assignment, opinion/proposal workflow, case pipeline, SLA, conversion/loss, and referrer relationship tracking.
- `platform-operations-and-commercial-proof`: Human verification/completeness/routing/coordination queues plus the event evidence needed to prove pilot response, conversion, repeat use, and economics.

### Modified Capabilities

None. This is a greenfield product definition.

## Impact

This change creates business, product, and behavioral contracts only. It adds no application code, database schema, technology choice, automated medical decision-making, or production integration. The refined founder document is `docs/REFINED_PRODUCT_BUSINESS_SPECIFICATION.md`; the earlier detailed functional/safety analysis remains in `docs/FUNCTIONAL_PRODUCT_SPECIFICATION.md` as background. Implementation requires a separately approved change after corridor, referrer, hospital-payment, workflow, and legal assumptions pass validation.
